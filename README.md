# HangulGo

**Free, no sign-up, learn Korean and survive a trip to Korea.**

Live app: https://azabumin2.github.io/hangulgo/

HangulGo is a Duolingo-style web app for learning to read Hangul (the Korean alphabet) and for handling real situations in Korea - asking for directions, navigating the subway, ordering food, shopping, and emergencies. Works on any phone or computer, installable as an app (Add to Home Screen), and works offline once loaded.

## Features

- **Level tree**: step-by-step lessons from basic consonants/vowels to combined syllables and words
- **Alphabet chart**: tap any letter or word to hear it pronounced
- **Survival phrasebook (new)**: 39 practical phrases across 6 categories (greetings, directions, public transit, restaurants, shopping, emergencies), each with Korean text, romanization, translation, and audio playback
- **Daily quests & streaks**: light gamification to keep you coming back
- **4 languages**: English, 日本語, 中文, 한국어 - the whole UI switches, not just the lesson content
- **Installable PWA**: add it to your home screen like a native app, no App Store needed

## Who it's for

Anyone visiting Korea who wants to read signs and survive the subway, and anyone starting to learn Hangul from zero. No account, no ads, no payment - just open the link.

## Tech

Single-file static web app (HTML/CSS/JS), no backend, hosted free on GitHub Pages.
